C. Additional Consequences of Refuting ETH Theorem 1.1 gives a circuit lower bound and subexponential-time algorithms for inverting Boolean maps with constant output locality. The fixed-density case gives the quantitative circuit lower bound in Corollary 1.4. Both circuit arguments use only the decision version of our 3-SAT algorithm. Exact preimage counting uses its full counting guarantee. C.1. Circuit lower bounds for ENP. For the qualitative bound below, write SIZE(O(n)) for the languages having nonuniform circuits with O(n) bounded-fan-in AND, OR, and NOT gates. Corollary C.1. We have ENP SIZE(O(n)). Proof. Williams proved that if ETH is false, then ENP does not have linear-size circuits [Wil13, Theorem 6.1]. Apply this implication to the deterministic subexponential-time 3-SAT algorithm supplied by Theorem 1.1. The connection is an instance of deriving circuit lower bounds from faster satisfiability algorithms. To encode a Boolean circuit with s gates and n inputs, introduce a variable for each gate, enforce its value from its inputs, and add a unit clause requiring the output bit to equal 1. The resulting 3-CNF has O(n + s) variables and clauses and is satisfiable exactly when the circuit is. Our theorem therefore decides satisfiability of linear-size circuits in 2o(n) time. Williams's argument combines such a speedup with hypothetical small circuits for witnesses to contradict a nondeterministic time hierarchy. The corollary gives a language in ENP without a linear-size circuit family; it does not assert a superpolynomial circuit lower bound for NP. Proof of Corollary 1.4.
